Local Dining Guide & Decision Science

How DinnerDecider Solves the "What Should We Eat Tonight?" Paradox

Research into everyday decision fatigue shows that the average couple or household spends over 130 hours a year debating what to have for dinner. After a long workday, scrolling through thousands of delivery listings on Uber Eats or DoorDash triggers choice overload—a cognitive bottleneck where having too many options makes every choice feel less satisfying. DinnerDecider combines real-time neighborhood restaurant discovery with structured game mechanics so you can go from hungry and indecisive to ordering in under 30 seconds.

🎡

1. The Local Restaurant Roulette Wheel

Unlike generic food spinners that only display words like "Pizza" or "Tacos," our wheel connects directly to verified local establishments around your postal code or GPS coordinates. Filter by budget tier ($ to $$$$), maximum distance in kilometers, and dining mode (Delivery, Takeout/Pickup, or Dine-In Patio).

💌

2. The 3-2-1 Partner Shortlist Method

Relationship psychologists recommend the 3-1 Rule for ending dinner stalemates: Person A selects 3 nearby restaurants they would genuinely enjoy, and texts the shareable shortlist link to Person B. Person B picks the single winner from those 3 cards—zero account signup or app install required.

⚔️

3. Head-to-Head Elimination & Craving Quiz

When a random spin doesn't feel right, switch to the Dinner Face-Off bracket. Comparing two real dishes side-by-side forces your intuition to surface what you're actually craving, while our 3-question diagnostic matches comfort food, quick bites, or healthy macro bowls to your exact appetite.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does DinnerDecider find restaurants near my exact postal code? ▼

When you enter a postal code, city, or tap "Use GPS," DinnerDecider geocodes your coordinates and queries OpenStreetMap's live Overpass restaurant directory alongside our curated local dining database. It calculates real Haversine straight-line distances in kilometers and generates verified Google Maps links using each restaurant's exact street address.

Can I add my own favorite local takeout spots to the wheel? ▼

Yes! Open the "My Spots" tab and click "Add Custom Spot." Enter the restaurant's name, street address, cuisine, and your go-to dish. Your custom spots are saved privately in your browser's local storage and immediately join the Roulette Wheel and Face-Off tournament.

How does the "Send Top 3 to Partner" link work without an account? ▼

When you select 3 restaurants in the Partner Shortlist modal, DinnerDecider encodes those 3 choices directly into a clean URL parameter (?shortlist=...). When you text or WhatsApp that link to your partner, their browser automatically opens in Partner Veto Mode so they can tap the final winner in one click.
