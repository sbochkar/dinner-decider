About DinnerDecider

DinnerDecider ends the "I don't know, you pick" argument. Tell it where you are and it turns the real restaurants around you into a quick game.

What it does

You can spin a wheel, take a short craving quiz, or run a face-off between two spots until one wins. Filters for dine-in, takeaway, dietary needs, cuisine and distance narrow things down first. You can also send a shortlist to your partner so they get the final say.

How decisions work

The wheel and face-off are random picks from the places that match your filters. The quiz scores places by cuisine and style, with a little randomness so you do not always get the same answer. Nothing is sponsored, and no restaurant can pay to appear or rank higher.

Local gems

Our Local gems pages are short lists of favourite spots in Ontario cities, picked by hand. They are editorial opinion, not paid placements. Each pick is checked against OpenStreetMap data so the address and contact details are real, and picks that no longer match a listed place are left off.

Where the data comes from

Restaurant locations, cuisines, hours and contact details come from OpenStreetMap, which is made by volunteers and available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). We rebuild our copy of the Ontario data every week. OpenStreetMap does not have ratings or reviews, so we do not show any.

Who runs it

DinnerDecider is a small hobby project run from Ontario, Canada. If something is wrong, or you know a place we should feature, get in touch.
