Local gems in Toronto

Downtown & west end

Downtown Toronto has more restaurants than anyone could try in a lifetime, which is exactly why picking one is so hard. These are the long-running favourites we'd send a friend to first — a mix of cheap-and-cheerful classics and a couple of special-occasion splurges.

Updated November 2026 · Editor's picks, not paid placements
