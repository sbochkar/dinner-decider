Local gems in Scarborough

Agincourt, Scarborough Town Centre & beyond

Scarborough is where a lot of Toronto actually eats: Hakka and Cantonese in Agincourt, Sri Lankan and South Indian along Lawrence and Markham Road, Caribbean all over. It's spread out and unglamorous, and that's the point — these are the places people drive across the city for.
