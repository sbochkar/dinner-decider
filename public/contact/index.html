Contact

The best way to reach us is email: hello@dinnerdecider.ca

What to write to us about

  • Closed or moved restaurants. Tell us the name and city. The fix is best made on OpenStreetMap, and we will pick it up in the next weekly refresh. We will also remove it from any Local gems page.
  • Corrections to anything we wrote on the site.
  • Suggestions for a place or a city we should add to Local gems, or a feature you would like.
  • Privacy requests, such as access to or correction of personal information. See our Privacy policy.
  • Business enquiries. Gems are never for sale, but we are happy to hear from you.

This is a one-person project, so replies can take a few days.

Restaurant data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Hours and details are community-maintained — double-check before you head out.
