Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

DinnerDecider has no accounts and does not ask you to sign up. This page explains what happens to your information.

Stored in your browser

Your chosen location (coordinates and a label), your filters, and the restaurants you save are stored in your browser's local storage so the app remembers them next time. This stays on your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site settings. Skipped restaurants are kept only for the current visit.

Sent to our server

When you search for an address or use "Use my location", your browser sends the search text or map coordinates (rounded to roughly 100 metres) to our server, which asks the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to turn it into an address or place name. Results may be cached for a short time. We do not link these requests to you or build a profile of you. Restaurant data itself is static files that your browser downloads for the area around you.

Shortlist links you share contain only restaurant IDs and an optional first name that you type.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process standard technical information such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Advertising

We may show ads on this site through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google handles data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We hold very little, but you are welcome to ask. Email hello@dinnerdecider.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

DinnerDecider is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
